================================================================================
CSE 220: Signals and Linear Systems
Practice Lab Problem 02: Fourier Series Synthesis & Reconstruction Error
================================================================================

Problem Statement:
A continuous periodic triangular waveform x(t) with fundamental period T0 = 4 
(f0 = 0.25 Hz) is defined over one period t in [-2, 2] as:
    x(t) = 1 - |t|   for |t| <= 1
    x(t) = 0         for 1 < |t| <= 2

Tasks:
1. Sample one fundamental period over t_period in [-2, 2] with 1000 samples.
2. Compute the complex Fourier Series coefficients c_n for harmonic indices 
   n in [-N, N] using numerical integration:
       c_n = (1 / T0) * \int_{-T0/2}^{T0/2} x(t) * exp(-j * 2 * pi * n * f0 * t) dt
3. Synthesize and reconstruct the signal over 3 periods t_eval in [-6, 6] using:
       x_hat_N(t) = \sum_{n=-N}^{N} c_n * exp(j * 2 * pi * n * f0 * t)
4. Evaluate the reconstruction for harmonic limits N in {3, 7, 25, 50}.
5. For each value of N, compute the Mean Squared Reconstruction Error:
       MSE_recon = (1 / M) * \sum_{m=1}^{M} |x(t_m) - x_hat_N(t_m)|^2
6. Plot the original repeating waveform against x_hat_N(t) to visualize Gibbs 
   phenomenon and convergence rate.
